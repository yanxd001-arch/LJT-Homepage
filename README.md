## About

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, advised by Professor Junxian He. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with interests in LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and Interpretability.
